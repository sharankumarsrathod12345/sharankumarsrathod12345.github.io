The business question

How do reported salaries differ across roles and countries, and which skills appear in the available job postings?

From postings to comparisons

The salary dashboard uses charts, formulas and dynamic controls. The companion analysis documents Power Query preparation, a Power Pivot relationship on job ID, PivotTables and DAX median-salary measures.

Select a role and geography before comparing salary distributions. Read the usable record count alongside each result: missing salaries and differences in seniority can affect the comparison.

A decision this supports

Form questions about the skills and roles represented in this historical sample. Use a fresh dataset and comparable job groups before making a present-day career or hiring decision.

Evidence and limits

Both workbooks and build notes are published. Exact original dataset URLs and reuse terms still need confirmation, and numerical workbook reconciliation has not been recorded in this review. A skill–salary association does not establish that learning a skill causes a salary increase.
