The business question

HR managers need to understand workforce composition and move from a summary to the records behind it. This project connects hiring, terminations, demographics and compensation with a filterable employee list.

Inside the dashboard

  • Summary view with headcount KPIs, hiring and termination trends, department comparisons and locations.
  • Demographic views covering gender, age, education and performance.
  • Income comparisons by education and gender, plus age and salary by job title.
  • Employee-details view with column filters, status, salary and employment length.
  • Navigation, show/hide filters and export controls within the Tableau workbook.

What the analysis supports

Use the summary to locate workforce concentrations, compare department staffing and explore compensation patterns. Then inspect the employee records for the selected segment.

The dataset contains seven departments. Operations is the largest with 2,718 records. New York state accounts for 6,270 records and is classified as HQ by the workbook.

Scope & limitations

This is a portfolio demonstration using generated employee data, not a paid-client engagement. Salary and demographic patterns reflect synthetic assumptions and cannot establish real-world discrimination or causal relationships.

Age is calculated from birthdate to TODAY() using year-boundary differences; tenure uses the hire date and either TODAY() or the termination date. These are not exact completed birthday or anniversary calculations, and dynamic values may differ from saved screenshots. Age groups are <25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54 and 55+.

Terminations divided by all records is not an annual turnover rate. Historical headcount would require a separate as-of-date definition.
