04 / CASE STUDY

Recommendation: test before reallocating

Prioritize a controlled test of the higher-spend, recent segment.

Its observed response rate makes it a candidate for testing, not a proven source of incremental lift.

Randomize eligible profiles into comparable treatment and control groups. Define the offer, observation period and success metric in advance. Obtain campaign costs and incremental revenue before judging profitability or ROI.

For the lower-spend, less-recent group, investigate offer relevance and contact economics before committing more budget.

05 / CASE STUDY

Scope & limitations

There is no customer ID, so exact-row deduplication is an assumption rather than proof of duplicate people. The sample is simulated; currency and the timing of spending relative to campaigns are not established. MntTotal excludes gold products.

Observed segment differences are descriptive. They do not establish campaign causality, incremental lift or client revenue improvement.
