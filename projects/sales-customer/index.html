01 / CASE STUDY

The business question

Sales managers need more than a total. They need to compare periods, identify where performance differs, and move from an overview to the relevant products or customers.

This portfolio project brings those questions into two connected Tableau dashboards.

02 / CASE STUDY

My contribution

  • Built sales and customer views using customer, location, order and product data.
  • Presented year-over-year KPI comparisons and monthly trends.
  • Added product and geographic filters, year selection and dashboard navigation.
  • Included weekly sales and profit views, customer order-frequency distribution and top customers by profit.

04 / CASE STUDY

What the analysis supports

The dashboard supports performance reviews and targeted investigation. A high-sales product can still have weak profitability; a weekly value below average is a prompt to investigate, not automatically a performance failure.

For a client adaptation, I would agree KPI definitions and reporting periods first, reconcile source totals, and then build the views around the decisions the team makes.

05 / CASE STUDY

Scope & limitations

This is descriptive portfolio work, not evidence of a measured business improvement. Customer purchasing frequency is not a formal retention or churn metric. Before business use, distinct order/customer counts, source totals and missing or zero prior-period behavior need validation.
