SQL SERVER / PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

Advanced SQL Analytics

Which categories drive sales value, and which drive unit volume? Explore product mix, repeat purchasing and monthly customer cohorts.

Explore the repository ↗

What the sales mix reveals

In the historical practice dataset, bikes account for 96.46% of recorded sales value, while accessories account for 59.77% of units. A manager reviewing sales value alone would miss the category responsible for most units sold.

For assortment planning, compare the two measures side by side. A decision about stocking or profitability would also require inventory levels and historical costs.

Following customers over time

The advanced analysis uses CTEs, window aggregates and LAG to compare periods and products. Annual repeat purchasing and monthly cohorts examine how customers return after their first observed purchase.

Two reusable SQL reports

The customer view summarizes sales, distinct orders, recency and spending segments by customer. The product view summarizes sales, units, customer counts and performance groups by product.

How to read the numbers

The repository reports 60,398 sales rows across 27,659 distinct orders. Nineteen rows lack an order date. The first and last calendar years are partial, and the source does not specify a currency. The figures describe the supplied CSV snapshot.
