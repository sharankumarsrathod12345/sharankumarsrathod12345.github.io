The business question

How do transaction activity, sales channels and delivery durations vary across products and customer groups?

From data to reporting

The repository provides a source workbook, an interactive dashboard workbook, a screenshot and an animated walkthrough. The reporting view combines orders, quantity, total amount, ratings and delivery duration.

Review a channel or product segment, then compare its delivery duration and customer ratings. Reset selections before comparing the dashboard with the source.

A decision this supports

Identify segments worth investigating for service delays or unusual sales patterns. Agree a delivery target before classifying performance as good or poor; an average alone cannot establish a missed service target.

Evidence and limits

The workbook and walkthrough demonstrate the reporting experience. Source-to-dashboard numerical reconciliation is not yet documented. Confirm order versus order-line grain, currency, reporting period and missing ratings before using totals for a business decision. No inventory-health, conversion-rate or measured business-impact claim is made.
